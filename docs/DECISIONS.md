# Product decisions

The project's current decisions, consolidated Nov 10, 2026. These win where [SPEC.md](SPEC.md) differs. Superseded layouts and implementation evidence live in [HISTORY.md](HISTORY.md) and git history. Detailed tool arguments are in [DEVELOPING.md](DEVELOPING.md); approved test seams are in [TESTING.md](TESTING.md).

## Performance and scope

- **Documented caps, local checks** (Nov 10, 2026). After considering a performance Action, the maintainer chose documentation: "that might be overkill for us … update everything … when we're working through a different feature … it's read … we have performance caps … we don't want to bloat the software." Read [PERFORMANCE.md](PERFORMANCE.md) before feature work and follow its before/after and correctness gate. Keep the existing caps; record misses and measured tradeoffs instead of quietly raising them. No new runner, Action or pre-push hook.
- **One personal Mac app** (Oct 20, 2026). All USD, one user, local data. Life started early by request; an iPhone/iCloud companion and a later personal agent are possibilities, not the current build scope. No holdings-level investment breakdown: "just get whatever SimpleFIN gives us right now" (Nov 9).

## Frame and appearance

- **Workbench** (Nov 9–10, 2026): "I'm more focused on the design aspect … all the same information … in this kind of design theme and format." Floating sidebar, top-bar search, shared cards/rows, Review page and canvas Settings. Native menus, shortcuts and sheets stay; Claude tools stay as before, with no suggestions queue. One Workbench palette replaces the seven themes and Thread gradient; light/dark/matching the Mac and separate heading/text fonts remain, Geist by default. Allocation is by account class. The selected face's monospaced digits replace the proposed Geist Mono.
- **Current frame** (Nov 10): window buttons inside the sidebar panel, Settings row at its bottom, Review first, Notes under Context. The app opens on Money. Money's range is a top-bar segment strip; add account/import/sync/How this works share ⋯. Spending keeps month and Budget/Over time at the top; Edit budget, Fill in from history and Categories move into ⋯ because the row "seems cluttered". Settings' selector is centred over its forms. New goal/habit and Playbook/Notes Add stay in the top bar. The window opens at the display's visible size (Oct 29), with folding/narrow layouts rather than a full-screen Space.
- **Type, colour and restraint** (Oct 28–Nov 10): heading/text fonts are independent of appearance; no greeting. One accent marks interaction/selection. Progress is ink, habit days are green, waiting Review is amber, gains/income green and losses/outflow red. Debt is olive: owing isn't failure. Nonessential explanations become hover tips; facts to act on remain. Links underline when colour is too close to ink. Plain banners, no decorative strips. DESIGN defines the current tokens and contrast checks.
- **Icon** (Oct 28): Convergence, Keystone, Fold or Focus, with a separate icon colour or matching the accent. Fold keeps its shading. The older five marks are retired. The installed icon is drawn from saved preferences; see DEVELOPING for the Dock limitation.
- **Merchant logos** (Nov 10): "fetch once, keep local." Logos fill their circle. Optional logo.dev key in Settings > Data/Keychain; Google fallback, any size accepted. A bad key falls back without marking the merchant missing. Banks/brokers and shop-via-delivery names can resolve too; local shops retain initials. Requests use domains. Reminders have no round monograms; habits have name-derived icons.
- **Motion only on a switch** (Nov 10): charts grow in and the headline rolls after changing view/month, never on opening, hover or scroll. Spending Over time is the pilot; applying it to net worth remains later work. The plot is masked rather than rebuilt per frame; reduced-motion behavior and performance stay required. Charts ignore hover while scrolling (Oct 23–25).

## Money and review

- **Money follows the draft** (Nov 10): net worth with allocation beside it; expandable class rows with history/accounts in one opening; then Flexible left, Goals and Waiting on you tiles. Total/By class/Monthly stay in a small chart menu. Year-to-date and outside-retirement figures are in the headline tooltip. Goals' full list/bars stay on Goals. Allocation uses positive class totals; Debt in Accounts shows what's owed. The nearest unreached net worth target appears as a dashed line only when near the series and amounts aren't hidden.
- **Debt reads as owed** (Oct 23): positive on class/account rows and debt histories, negative for an overpaid card. Storage stays negative so net worth remains a sum.
- **Opening balances aren't growth** (Oct 30): linking an account adds its first balance to totals, not to changes, monthly reviews or goal pace, except money moved from another tracked account. Investing-only goals pace by contributions, with market change apart. Internal trades, including buys naming an ex-dividend date, aren't deposits/withdrawals.
- **Review every new transaction** (Oct 29, Workbench Nov 9): rules/guesses file new synced transactions, which count in the budget immediately and wait for confirmation. Changing a category teaches a merchant rule and refiles its other unconfirmed charges; imported history is reviewed. Row details explain current rules, not original provenance; pending guesses cannot explain themselves. Confirm selected/all and transfer decisions wait five seconds with Undo; leaving/quitting saves what's waiting and failures stay visible. Keyboard ↑↓/Space/Return/Esc applies while the list is focused.
- **Transactions summary** (Nov 10): one chosen month, this month by default, plus five preceding months in green/red bars. Figures/categories reflect matching filters independently of pagination; unfiltered reads cover those months rather than all history. Goal-paid spending is included here. Day headers say spent/received, excluding transfers and unfiled deposits; transfer-only days say so.
- **Goals list and page** (Oct 22, Nov 9): attention order, status in words, progress/minimum, needed versus actual pace and arrival; detail beside the list when wide, page/back when narrow. Reached goals move to Reached, except a newly reached one stays for that visit and fills its bar once. Targets, projections, chain, reward/claim/photo and retirement remain. Goals end as achieved or retired, never deleted; no separate Milestones screen or Total vs Accessible chart. An undated goal reads growing/not growing (Oct 31).

## Budget and Pay

- **Flex budget** (Oct 20–31): fixed, flexible and irregular spending plus separate savings lines. Spending leads with flexible money left; no daily allowance (Oct 22: a daily figure was overkill). Budget and Over time remain; Compare/Heatmap are gone. Category charges expand in place, one at a time; monthly transactions start collapsed and remember their state. History can create a starting plan.
- **Flexible is the remainder** (Oct 31: "decide one behavior … and apply it to every month"): with income planned, flexible is income less fixed/irregular bills and savings, never negative. It is shown, not typed; Claude cannot set it. Typed flexible is only for a month with no planned income. When all income is assigned, say nothing (Nov 10); otherwise show unassigned money or overplanning. Savings aren't expenses; only savings accounts added to the plan count in its lines, net of money taken back. Brokerage-to-Roth and payroll saving aren't new budget saving.
- **Month plans** (Oct 29): Budget can plan a year ahead, Over time ends at the current month. Edits apply onward or one month only, with earlier months intact. Category roles are shared across months. In-place planning and row menus stay alongside Edit budget in ⋯. A bill no longer planned remains as an unbudgeted line (Nov 7); whole-dollar remaining values avoid "$0 over" beside Paid. Categories edits kind/name/merges.
- **Refunds, reimbursements, splits** (Oct 31): reimbursements reduce spending by what's owed back; splits count part by part. One-off is a flag, not an exclusion. Notes/flags/categories can be changed without changing amounts, dates or merchants; a single Claude refile doesn't teach a merchant rule. Transactions aren't deleted.
- **Goal-funded spending** (Nov 1): a cash-account goal can pay all or part of a charge. Keep it visible and marked in Spending/Over time, outside budget expenses, flexible pace and daily spent. Funding transfers don't reduce saving/savings rate. Until moved, subtract what the fund still owes from its progress and show it on the goal page; its pace excludes what it paid for. No money moves automatically.
- **Pay from settings and syncs** (Oct 31): "I will NOT import paystubs every paycheck. Everything per-paycheck must come from existing syncs; settings change ~once a year." Salary/schedule/known payday and contribution settings define expected checks; synced Paychecks deposits within three days establish net pay. The budget plans two biweekly checks or four weekly, leaving extra checks, bonuses and stock out. A paystub import only fills settings. A 401(k)'s nearby buys establish contributions split as the settings split them; absent buys are estimated, as stock-plan amounts always are. Later syncs reach 30 days back from the newest transaction.
- **Savings rate** (Oct 31): all net transfers from everyday accounts into savings/investing, budgeted or not, plus payday 401(k), match and stock, divided by salary plus match. "Net out a transfer and its reversal within the same month … so the rate isn't overstated." Expected bonuses/vests stay separate and can be estimates. Pay and investing contributions use these settings/synced checks, not a requirement for recurring stubs.

## Life and context

- **Current Life layout** (Nov 10): Calendar day tiles replace the hour grid. Two-week habit strips with today's check sit beside To do/Admin, then This quarter/Coming up, then Vision/bets/Living List. Playbook Today lives on Playbook only. No money on Life (Oct 28). Real Calendar events retain their calendar colours and open Calendar; Settings chooses visible calendars. Free time imposes no booking hours: the client decides what suits the user (Oct 27).
- **Reminders owns the lists** (Oct 25–30): This Week and due-by-Saturday tasks (except This Quarter), overdue first; repeats join only the day before due and stay overdue. Admin/Errands has its own part of To do. This Quarter remains separate even in deadline week and can follow net worth/a money goal, with progress and a reward. Coming Up/Long Term stays under it; Living List sits with Vision, whose yearly bets are separate editable lines. Hidden lists are excluded, shopping starts hidden, redundant list-name prefixes are dropped. Ticks save after two seconds, cancellable before then. Resolutions, Notes paste and a separate Vision screen are retired; legacy data remains backed up.
- **List names are settings** (Nov 13, 2026): the lists Compass gives a job to (this week, admin, this quarter, coming up, living list, inbox, Playbook repeats) are found by name. The standard names are the defaults; Settings › Data takes other names, several per job, so nobody has to rename their lists to use Life.
- **Habits and repeats** (Oct 28–Nov 2): every daily repeat becomes a linked seven-day habit, carrying up to 90 days of phone ticks. Today's Compass tick completes its due reminder; unticking/history edits don't reopen it. Archive to stop tracking it; a linked habit takes the reminder's title. Habits has tally/day grid/trends, while Life holds check-ins. No missed-week rule picker/warning. Weeks start Sunday (Nov 1); stored weekday numbers don't change.
- **Playbook** (Oct 30–Nov 10): picks, weekday/everyday routines and notes, plus sizes and brand exceptions; fitness is what works, not a workout log; no photos. Searchable Today/sizes/area list with selected details, no area filter. Today includes routines and repeats due within two days; an off-day routine previews its next session/step count. Repeating entries link to plain reminders in Repetitive Tasks, due from last done. Phone completion/rescheduling carries back; deletion there isn't recreated without Put Back. The last-bought shop/history block is removed (Nov 10: "kind of irrelevant"); a Restock list hint remains. Entries/sizes can be edited/deleted locally or via Claude with undo.
- **Notes** (Oct 31–Nov 1): "durable rules and reasons" not figures available elsewhere; topic/title/body, at most 1,000 characters and case-insensitively unique titles. Local and MCP read/write/delete, logged with undo. Context in the sidebar, independent of which agent the user connects. MCP instructions ask the client to read notes for planning/advice and avoid copied figures; that convention isn't enforced.

## Lock, storage and Claude

- **Three lock modes** (Oct 27): Off; When it opens (default); After sleep or idle. Touch ID or Mac-password unlock; Lock Now (⌘L) in modes that lock. The menu bar panel (Nov 2: "I don't have a notch") shows today's reminders, flexible left and habits; closing its main window keeps Compass running and locks in modes that lock. No figures in the menu bar icon, no private panel content while locked. Quick capture/global shortcut/fake notch are out.
- **How it works** (Nov 2): a Settings tab plus ⓘ for the current screen, "Plain rules + formulas." `Guide.swift` changes when the rules/numbers do. Settings has the same lock gate as other screens.
- **A failed save says so** (Nov 13, 2026): an edit that can't be saved shows one plain alert ("Compass couldn't save that change") instead of vanishing; background saves are logged. The budget editor stays open with what was typed.
- **Safe store and backups** (Oct 31): reject unknown models/fields to prevent older builds dropping data; JSON backup daily, two weeks retained. No in-app restore yet. Never launch an arbitrary bundle-ID match; install/relaunch by path.
- **One local MCP writer** (Oct 27–31): the running app serves Core-derived answers through its EventKit permissions, loopback-only with a Keychain key, separately enabled/off by default. The visual lock doesn't gate the authorized endpoint. Claude Code connects directly, desktop through the bundled bridge that opens its exact app in the background. No SimpleFIN calls through MCP.
- **Writes require chat confirmation and undo** (Oct 31: "Claude can maintain everything in Compass through chat. Every write is logged in `changes` and undoable. Claude confirms with me before writing; the tools don't need their own confirmation step"). Coverage: goals/retirement/rewards; reminders/repeats; habits/check-ins; Vision/bets; Playbook/sizes; notes; pay settings/expected items; account create/rename/archive and manual balances; merchant rules/categories; manual-account transactions, notes/flags/refiling/splits; budget; calendar events. Synced balances and history, transaction amounts/dates/merchants, and sync stay read-only. Nothing moves money. Manual transaction creation on synced accounts is refused.
- **Write limits**: goals aren't deleted or manually marked reached. Manual-account creation undoes only while it has its opening balance and no transactions; balance undo removes only the value Claude wrote. A linked habit is archived rather than deleted, and Claude check-in/untick leaves its reminder alone. A repeating reminder can be edited/completed/reopened but not deleted because undo can't restore its schedule. Calendar creation/deletion is limited to the app-created Compass calendar and deletion to its own events. Other visible writable calendars allow edits/moves after confirmation, one occurrence of a repeat at a time; hidden/read-only calendars, invitations and events with guests are refused. Undo refuses to overwrite later changes. Activity records stay local and outside backups because undo can involve live EventKit objects.
