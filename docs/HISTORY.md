# History

How Compass got here: milestone states, measurements and the acceptance checklist. Moved out of CLAUDE.md on Oct 23, 2026 so it isn't loaded every session.

## Where things stand

M1–v1 were built on stacked branches and merged to `main`. **`main` is always the newest version**: every change lands on `main` and is pushed to `origin/main` straight away, and the copy installed in `/Applications` is built from `main`. `next-batch` and the milestone branches are history.

## M1 acceptance checklist (Oct 20, 2026 baseline)

These steps predate the three lock modes. To repeat the sleep check, select After sleep or idle; the current default is When it opens. Current live acceptance remains in the design-plan files.

1. Launch the app. The lock screen appears, and cancelling Touch ID shows no numbers.
2. The Mac password fallback unlocks it.
3. Add a manual account at $1,000. Net worth shows $1,000.
4. Paste the demo token and sync. The demo accounts appear, and net worth equals their sum plus $1,000.
5. Sync again. Nothing is duplicated.
6. Turn off Wi-Fi and sync. A clear error appears, not a crash.
7. Quit and relaunch. The data is still there, and the app locks again.
8. Sleep the Mac and wake it. The app is locked.
